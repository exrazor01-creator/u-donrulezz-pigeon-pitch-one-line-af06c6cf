## Why it fits the brief

1. **On subject:** a one-line elevator pitch from a city pigeon to investors — product, customer, and ask in one breath.
2. **Funny:** the punchline lands on the last words — "$2M in seed" means actual birdseed; investor vocabulary collides with a pigeon's literal diet.
3. **Original:** a statue-rating app for pigeons is a twist nobody has met before.
4. **Pigeon voice, city life:** cooing, statues, landing spots, seed — this pitch could only come from a pigeon.
5. **Tight:** every word earns its place.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
